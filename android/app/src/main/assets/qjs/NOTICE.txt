QuickJS (Android binaries)
--------------------------
Bundled `qjs` executables (arm64-v8a, x86_64) are taken from
youtubedl-android 0.18.1 (`libqjs.so` jniLibs), originally built for
running yt-dlp YouTube JS challenges on Android.

Upstream: https://github.com/yausername/youtubedl-android
QuickJS: https://bellard.org/quickjs/ / https://github.com/quickjs-ng/quickjs
